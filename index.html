“The Temasek Club Switzerland (TCS) plays an extremely important role in helping overseas Singaporeans in Switzerland to stay connected to one another and to Singapore.

TCS has done very well in organising a range of meaningful events and activities that enable Singaporeans to get to know each other, to support each other, and to create a home away from home.

The Singapore Mission in Geneva is pleased to support TCS in its efforts to foster a greater sense of belonging to our Little Red Dot.”

Ambassador Tan Hung Seng

TCS Honorary Advisor
