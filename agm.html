Annual General Meeting Docs

An Annual General Meeting (AGM) is a yearly gathering where members of the Singaporeans in Switzerland group review the past year's activities and finances, vote on proposals, and elect the committee for the coming term. It's also a chance for members to catch up, welcome newcomers, and strengthen the community.
